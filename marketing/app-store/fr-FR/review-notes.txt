Altio connects vacation rental owners with cleaning and maintenance professionals. Payments are for physical services performed outside the app. No digital goods or subscriptions are sold.

Use the owner demo credentials supplied in App Store Connect. This account contains two fictional properties and two assigned cleaning missions. Review route: Accueil > Logements > select a property; Planning > Missions > select a mission to view its details and messages; Compte > legal information.

Account deletion is available in Compte > Supprimer mon compte. The populated demonstration account has ongoing missions, which prevent deletion; a newly registered account without ongoing work can be used to inspect deletion.

Camera and photo library access support property and intervention photographs. Location helps find nearby services. Notifications support mission follow-up.
